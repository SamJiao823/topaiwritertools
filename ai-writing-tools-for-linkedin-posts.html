What makes a LinkedIn post work?

  • A personal hook in the first line
  • One real insight or lesson
  • Specific examples, not generic advice
  • A question to drive comments
  • Line breaks for readability

How do you prompt AI to write a LinkedIn post?

Give AI: your insight + audience + desired tone + a personal example. Ask for a hook, body with line breaks, and a closing question. Edit for authenticity.

Does authenticity still matter with AI drafts?

LinkedIn users detect generic AI content fast. Add a real story, real numbers, or a genuine opinion. AI drafts; you make it authentic.
