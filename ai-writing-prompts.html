Blog & Article Writing

📝 Blog Outline Generator
Blog "Act as an SEO content strategist. Create a detailed outline for a blog post targeting the keyword '[primary keyword].' The target audience is [audience]. Include: H2 and H3 headings, key points per section, suggested internal links, FAQ ideas (3-5 questions), and a meta description under 160 characters. The outline should be comprehensive but leave room for my unique perspective."
Best for: Starting any blog post with a strong structure
✍️ Full Blog Post Draft
Blog "Write a [word count]-word blog post about [topic]. Primary keyword: '[keyword]' — use it in the first 100 words and in one H2. Secondary keywords: [list]. Tone: [tone]. Audience: [audience]. Structure: hook → problem → solution → evidence → conclusion. Include transition sentences between sections. Avoid clichés. Write in active voice with short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)."
Best for: Full article generation
💡 Blog Intro Generator
Blog "Write 3 different opening paragraphs for a blog post about [topic]. Option 1: Start with a surprising statistic. Option 2: Start with a relatable problem. Option 3: Start with a bold statement. Each intro should be 60-100 words, include the keyword '[keyword]' naturally, and end with a sentence that leads into the next section."
🏁 Blog Conclusion Generator
Blog "Write a conclusion for a blog post about [topic]. Summarize the 3 key takeaways in one sentence each. End with a question to encourage comments. Include one sentence about why this topic matters now. Max 100 words. No 'in conclusion' or 'to wrap up.'"

SEO & Keyword Optimization

🔍 SEO Meta Description
SEO "Generate 5 meta descriptions for a blog post titled '[title].' Primary keyword: '[keyword].' Each must: be under 160 characters, include the keyword naturally, include a benefit or promise, and end without a period. Make them compelling enough to improve click-through rate."
📊 Content Gap Analysis
SEO "Compare my article [URL] with these competing articles [URLs]. Identify: 1) Topics my article covers that competitors don't. 2) Topics competitors cover that my article is missing. 3) Questions the top-ranking content raises but doesn't answer. 4) Suggest 3 specific additions that would make my article more comprehensive and improve its ranking potential."
🔗 Internal Link Suggestions
SEO "Based on this article about [topic], suggest 5 internal links to other pages on my website [list domains/pages]. For each suggestion, provide: the anchor text to use, the target page, and a one-sentence explanation of why the link adds value for the reader."
🏆 Title Tag Optimizer
SEO "Generate 8 title tag options for a page targeting '[keyword].' Each option must be under 60 characters, include the primary keyword as early as possible, and appeal to [search intent: informational/commercial/transactional]. Rank them from best to worst with a one-sentence rationale for the top pick."
📋 FAQ Generator for GEO
SEO "Generate 10 FAQ questions and answers about [topic] that would help this content appear in AI search results (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity). Each question should be something a real person would ask in natural language. Each answer should be 2-3 sentences, factual, and include the primary keyword where natural."
Best for: AEO / GEO optimization (AI search visibility)

Email Marketing

📧 Welcome Email Sequence
Email "Write a 3-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to [business type]. Email 1: Welcome + what to expect + free resource link. Email 2: Value/educational content related to signup reason. Email 3: First offer with social proof. Tone: [tone]. Each email: 80-120 words, one CTA, no spammy language. Subject lines included."
📬 Promotional Email
Email "Write a promotional email for [product/service]. The offer: [details]. Deadline: [date]. Audience: [segment]. Tone: [tone]. Structure: problem → solution → offer → urgency → CTA. Include: subject line, preview text, email body (80-100 words), CTA button text. No false urgency. Focus on value."
📨 Newsletter Content
Email "Write a newsletter for [topic/industry]. Include: 1) One insightful observation (2-3 sentences). 2) One useful tip (50 words). 3) One recommended resource. Opening line should make the reader feel like they're getting an insider perspective. Keep the tone conversational. Max 200 words total."

Social Media

🐦 Twitter/X Thread
Social "Write a Twitter/X thread about [topic]. Include 5-7 tweets. Tweet 1: Hook that stops the scroll. Middle tweets: Key insights with line breaks for readability. Last tweet: CTA + link to full article. Each tweet under 280 characters. Use plain language, no jargon."
💼 LinkedIn Post
Social "Write a LinkedIn post about [topic]. Opening: A personal observation or lesson learned (2 sentences). Body: The key insight with a specific example (3-4 sentences). Closing: A question to drive comments. Max 200 words. Use line breaks between sections. No hashtags in the body — add 3-5 at the bottom."
📸 Instagram Caption
Social "Write 3 Instagram caption options for a post about [topic]. Each caption: 80-120 words, conversational tone, one CTA (link in bio / comment / save). Include 5-8 relevant hashtags. The captions should stop the scroll in the first line."

Product Descriptions

🏪 Standard Product Description
Product "Write a [word count]-word product description for [product name]. Target customer: [who + pain point]. Lead with: [main benefit]. Include specs: [list]. Tone: [tone]. Avoid: 'premium,' 'high-quality,' 'amazing.' End with one sentence about why this solves their specific problem."
🛒 Feature → Benefit Converter
Product "Convert these product features into benefit-driven copy for [customer type]. For each feature, explain why it matters to them and what problem it solves. Features: [list specs]. Format: Feature → Benefit → Why-It-Matters in bullet points. Keep each benefit under 25 words."
📦 E-Commerce Bulk Description
Product "Using the same structure and tone, write short descriptions for these product variants. Only vary the variant-specific detail (color, size, material). Keep each description under 50 words. Variants: [list with specs]. Tone: [tone]."

Editing & Rewriting

✂️ Clarity Editor
Edit "Rewrite the following text for maximum clarity. Shorten long sentences. Replace jargon with plain language. Remove filler words ('very,' 'really,' 'actually'). Keep the original tone and key information. Text: [paste]"
🎭 Tone Shifter
Edit "Rewrite this text to be more [desired tone: professional/casual/persuasive/empathetic]. Keep all key information intact. Change only the tone and word choice. Show both the original and revised version. Text: [paste]"
📏 Active Voice Converter
Edit "Rewrite this text to use active voice wherever possible. Identify passive constructions and suggest active alternatives. Explain why each change improves readability. Text: [paste]"
🚫 AI Filler Finder
Edit "Scan this text for common AI writing patterns: unnecessary em dashes, 'not only... but also' constructions, 'in today's digital landscape,' 'it's important to note that,' and redundant contrasting pairs. Flag each instance, explain why it's a tell, and suggest a simpler alternative. Text: [paste]"
📝 Readability Improver
Edit "Edit this text to improve readability for a general audience. Target reading level: 8th grade. Shorten sentences over 25 words. Replace complex words with simpler alternatives. Break long paragraphs into 2-3 sentence chunks. Keep the meaning and tone intact. Text: [paste]"

Content Strategy

🗓️ Content Calendar Generator
Strategy "Create a 30-day content calendar for [business/niche]. Include content types (blog, email, social), suggested topics, target keywords, and a brief rationale for each piece. Prioritize topics that serve both SEO and customer education goals. Format as a table."
🎯 Topic Cluster Planner
Strategy "Design a topic cluster strategy for the seed topic '[topic].' Identify: 1 pillar page topic that broadly covers the subject, and 8-12 cluster content topics that target specific long-tail keywords. For each cluster piece, suggest the keyword, search intent, and how it links back to the pillar page."
🏆 Competitor Content Audit
Strategy "Analyze the content strategy of [competitor URL]. Identify: their top-performing content types, content gaps (topics they don't cover), posting frequency, tone and style patterns, and 3 specific content opportunities my brand could exploit that they're missing."
🔄 Content Repurposing Plan
Strategy "I have a [type of content] about [topic]. Suggest 5 ways to repurpose it into different formats (social posts, email, video script, infographic outline, podcast outline). For each format, provide a 2-3 sentence summary adapting the core message."

Pro Tips for Better Results

🔥 Prompt Engineering Best Practices

  • Stack prompts in sequence: Outline → Section 1 → Section 2 → Polish → Metadata. Each step builds on the last.
  • Upload reference material: Paste your brand guide, past articles, or competitor examples as context before generating.
  • Iterate after the first output: The first prompt gets you 80%. A follow-up like "Make this more specific in paragraph 3" gets you to publishable.
  • Use real writing samples: Pasting your own paragraph as a "voice sample" outperforms describing tone with adjectives.
  • Be specific about audience: "Rewrite for technical project managers who skim on mobile" beats "Rewrite for a blog audience."
  • Match tool to task: Claude for natural long-form, Writesonic for SEO content, ChatGPT for versatility.
