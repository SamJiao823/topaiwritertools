Are AI-generated headlines any good?

Yes — for first drafts. AI headline generators follow the same patterns that have driven clicks for decades: numbers, curiosity gaps, and clear promises. Treat the output as a starting point: pick the strongest one, swap in a specific detail, and run it against two or three alternatives.
