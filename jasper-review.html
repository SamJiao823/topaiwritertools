What is Jasper AI?

Jasper (formerly Jarvis) is one of the most well-known AI writing tools on the market. Founded in 2021, it quickly became the go-to tool for marketers and content teams — before the rise of ChatGPT changed everything.

In 2026, Jasper positions itself as an enterprise AI content platform, targeting marketing teams rather than individual writers. It offers brand voice settings, team collaboration, and integrations with tools like Surfer SEO and Grammarly.

But here's the honest truth: Jasper's affiliate program is now closed to new members, and at $49/mo for the Creator plan, it's one of the most expensive options in the market.
