How did we test these cheap AI writing tools?

We spent 30 days using the budget end of the AI writing market. We wrote emails, blog sections, social posts, and product descriptions, then measured quality, editing time, and value per dollar.

Which cheap AI writing tool is strong value?

For short-form on a tight budget, Rytr at $7.50/mo is strong value in AI writing. It has templates, a plagiarism checker, and a forever-free plan to test before you pay. Quality is a step below premium tools, but at a third of the price, it's hard to beat.

If you write SEO content, the extra $12.50/mo for Writesonic is worth it — the built-in SEO tools save you more in editing time than the price difference.

And honestly, if you want a strong free option, Claude's free tier produces the most natural long-form writing of anything we tested — paid or free.

Which cheap AI writing tool should you pick?

  • Most budget-friendly: Rytr — $7.50/mo with a free plan
  • Free + strong quality: Claude — generous free tier
  • Cheap + SEO features: Writesonic — $20/mo

Which expensive AI writing tools should you avoid?

Jasper at $49/mo is excellent but 6x the price of Rytr. If you're a solo creator on a budget, you're paying for team features you'll never use. Start with a cheap tool and upgrade only when you outgrow it.
