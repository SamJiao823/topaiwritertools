How do you write social posts that convert?

  1. Give the AI your voice: Paste one of your strongest past posts as a style reference
  2. Specify the platform: "Instagram caption" beats "social media post"
  3. Set a constraint: Length, tone, one CTA, no emojis — whatever fits your brand
  4. Write hooks first: Ask for 5 first-line hooks, pick the strongest
  5. Repurpose: One long post → thread + carousel + email teaser
