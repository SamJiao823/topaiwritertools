The 90+ Marketing Templates: Use Them Without Sounding Generic

Copy.ai ships more than 90 marketing templates for ads, emails, social posts, landing pages, and more. The honest guidance: templates produce usable first drafts, not finished copy. The trap is using them raw — the output leans formulaic, and if a reader can spot the pattern, the copy is working against you. Treat each template as a structure, then rewrite the openings, inject your product specifics, and cut the filler. The most common mistake is pasting template output straight into a campaign; fill the inputs with real details and the quality jumps noticeably.

Brand Voice and Infobase: Your Consistency Layer

Two features do the heavy lifting for consistency. Brand Voice teaches the tool your tone: paste sample copy and it matches that style on later generations. Infobase is a knowledge base where you store product facts, positioning, and FAQs, so the AI stops inventing details about your business. Together they're the difference between generic AI copy and copy that sounds like your brand — set both up on day one rather than after you're already generating.

Workflow Automation: The Catch (Honest)

Here's the honest part. Copy.ai's workflow automation — building multi-step automated writing pipelines — is impressive and genuinely distinctive. But it only starts on the Growth plan at $1,000/month. The $29 Chat plan does not include it, and the jump from Chat to Growth is roughly 35x with no middle tier. If you're evaluating Copy.ai for automation specifically, price it as a $1,000/month tool, not a $29 one. This is the main reason we score Copy.ai 7/6 for GTM teams but flag it as poor value for solo users.
