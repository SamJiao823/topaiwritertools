Is QuillBot Free Actually Enough?

For light rewriting, yes. The 125-word limit per pass covers a sentence or short paragraph — the exact thing most people use QuillBot for. Two modes (Standard and Fluency) are enough to get unstuck on phrasing. The catch comes when you hit the limits in a real workflow: rewriting a full paragraph takes several passes, the summarizer stops at 1,200 words, and grammar checking is basic. If your need is "fix this one sentence," free is fine. If you're rewriting entire drafts, the caps get annoying fast.

Is Premium Worth $19.95/mo?

At the monthly price, rarely — $19.95 for a rewriter is steep when you can get a full AI writer (Rytr at $7.50) for less. But the annual rate changes the math: $8.33/mo for unlimited paraphrasing, all modes, and a proper summarizer is a fair price for students and writers who use the tool daily. Our read: if QuillBot is part of your weekly workflow, pay the annual rate and forget about it. If you're just occasionally stuck on a sentence, stay free.

QuillBot vs Grammarly: Different Jobs

The most common confusion is QuillBot vs Grammarly. They're not competitors in the same lane: Grammarly corrects grammar — it fixes mistakes and suggests better wording. QuillBot rewrites — it takes your sentence and produces a different version. If your problem is typos and comma splices, that's Grammarly. If your problem is "this sentence reads awkwardly, help me rephrase it," that's QuillBot. Our Grammarly vs QuillBot comparison walks through the choice in detail. You can run both free tiers before paying for either.
