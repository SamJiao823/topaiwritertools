What do people ask about AI Writing ROI Calculator: Words per Dollar?

Is an AI writing tool worth it for low volume?

If you write a few pieces a month, a free tier (ChatGPT, QuillBot) is often enough before paying for a dedicated tool.

Why does cost per word drop as I write more?

Because the plans are flat monthly fees — the subscription cost is fixed, so spreading it over more words lowers the unit cost.

Do these prices include editing time?

No. The subscription is the small cost; human review of output is the larger one and isn't in any calculator.

Which tool is cheapest per word?

At any real volume, Rytr at $7.50/month is the cheapest per 1,000 words of the four compared here.
