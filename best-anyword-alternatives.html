Why Look for Anyword Alternatives?

Anyword is a legitimate AI writing tool with a unique value proposition — its predictive performance analytics tell you how your copy will perform before you publish. For data-driven marketers, this is genuinely useful.

But there are three reasons you might want an alternative:

  • Price: $49/month is expensive compared to Rytr ($7.50) and Writesonic ($20)
  • Affiliate program pending: Our application is still under review, and there's no public affiliate program details
  • No free plan: Unlike Rytr's forever free plan, Anyword only offers a 7-day trial

If you're looking for the most affordable Anyword alternative that still delivers quality content, Rytr is our top pick. At $7.50/month for unlimited words, it costs 85% less than Anyword. The forever free plan (10K chars/month) lets you test it thoroughly before committing.
