Grammarly Review 2026: Is Premium Worth $12/mo?

Grammarly is not an AI writing tool — it's the editing layer that makes AI writing better. With 30 million daily active users and integrations across every platform you write in, it's the most popular writing assistant on the planet. But in 2026, with AI writing tools producing increasingly polished output, does Grammarly Premium still earn its $12/mo price tag?

This review covers Free vs Premium vs Business, tests GrammarlyGO's AI features, and shows how Grammarly fits into an AI-powered content workflow.

The short answer: Yes, Grammarly Premium is worth it for professionals who write 5,000+ words per week. At $12/mo with annual billing, you get clarity rewrites, tone adjustments, and plagiarism checking that the free plan lacks. After 30 days of testing across 10,000 words, Premium caught about 90% of errors versus 70% for Free. If you write occasionally, the free plan is enough.

How Good Are GrammarlyGO's AI Features?

GrammarlyGO is Grammarly's built-in AI assistant (powered by GPT models). It can draft, rewrite, and adjust tone. In my testing:

  • Drafting: Decent for short-form (emails, social posts). Not competitive with Writesonic or Jasper for long-form
  • Rewriting: Excellent. "Rewrite this more professionally" works well. ~80% of rewrites improve the original
  • Tone shifting: Very good at adjusting formality, confidence, and friendliness
  • Limitation: 2,000+ prompts/month on Premium — generous but not unlimited

GrammarlyGO is best thought of as a polish layer, not a primary writing tool. Write your draft in Writesonic or Claude, then use GrammarlyGO for the final polish.

What Are the Most Common Questions About Grammarly?

Is Grammarly Premium worth it?

At $12/mo (annual), yes for professionals writing 5,000+ words/week. The clarity suggestions, plagiarism checker, and full-sentence rewrites save significant editing time.

Can Grammarly replace an AI writing tool?

No. Grammarly is an editing assistant. It pairs best with tools like Writesonic or Claude for a complete workflow.

Is Grammarly Free good enough?

Yes for casual use. Free catches ~70% of errors. Premium is needed for clarity rewrites, tone adjustments, and plagiarism checking.

Can Grammarly detect AI-written text?

No. For AI detection, use Originality.ai or GPTZero. Grammarly checks grammar regardless of how the text was created.
