QuillBot calls itself the rewriter, and that narrow focus is the whole point: it is not a general-purpose AI writer and not a grammar checker — it rephrases sentences and condenses text. This walkthrough covers every major feature step by step. One honest note up front: QuillBot is not one of the eight tools in our 30-day benchmark, so the feature descriptions and limits below come from QuillBot's official documentation, not a hands-on test.

1. Getting Started: The Free Plan

Head to quillbot.com and the free plan is available immediately — no credit card, no trial timer. The free tier is a permanent freemium, not a limited demo. Your caps: 125 words per paraphrase pass, two modes, a 1,200-word summarizer, and basic grammar checking.

The 125-word cap is per pass, which changes how you work. You don't get 125 words per day — you get 125 words each time you run the paraphraser. For a long paragraph, split it into sentences or short clauses and run each one. It's slower than unlimited, but it works, and it keeps the free plan genuinely usable for occasional rewriting.

2. Paraphrasing: The Core Feature

Paste a sentence you're stuck on, pick a mode, and QuillBot returns a rephrased version. The free plan gives you two modes (Standard and Fluency); Premium unlocks the rest, including Formal, Simple, and Creative. The synonym slider controls how aggressively QuillBot swaps vocabulary — keep it low and the sentence barely changes, push it high and it reads very differently. That slider is the real power control: low for light polish, high for a full restatement.

One honest warning that QuillBot's own guidance makes clear: rewritten text can be flagged by modern AI detectors, especially when the paraphrasing is aggressive. Use QuillBot to improve your own writing and to see alternative phrasings, not to hide AI authorship. Teachers and editors do run detection tools, and a suspiciously "humanized" paragraph is exactly what they look for. For more on that trade-off, see our how to paraphrase with AI guide.

3. Summarizer: Reading Long Text Faster

The summarizer condenses long text into key points. The free tier handles up to 1,200 words at a time — enough for an abstract or a section of a paper. Paste the passage, choose the summary length, and read the condensed version. Premium lifts the cap, which matters if you regularly condense whole chapters or articles. Treat it as a reading aid, not as a substitute for reading the source yourself.

4. Grammar Checker: Basic, Not Best-in-Class

QuillBot's grammar checker catches typos, punctuation issues, and simple errors on the free plan. It's honest but not deep — it won't explain tone, style, or subtle sentence flow the way a dedicated checker does. If grammar mistakes are your main problem, that's a different tool's job: see how to use Grammarly or our Grammarly vs QuillBot comparison.

5. Translator and Citation Generator: Premium Features

Two more tools live in the same workspace, and both are only fully unlocked on Premium. The translator renders text in other languages, which helps when you're working with non-English sources. The citation generator builds references in APA, MLA, and Chicago from a title or URL — a real time-saver for reference lists, but verify each entry before submission, because no generator is infallible.

7. Free vs Premium: How to Choose

Premium is $19.95/mo billed monthly or $8.33/mo billed annually — about $100/year, and the annual rate is the only one worth considering. Our take: if you occasionally unstick a sentence, the free plan is enough and stays free forever. If you rewrite drafts every day or hit the 125-word cap regularly, the annual rate removes every limit for less than a coffee a week. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start using QuillBot for free?

Go to quillbot.com and paste text into the paraphraser. No credit card required — the free plan is a permanent freemium tier, not a time-limited trial.

Is 125 words per paraphrase pass enough?

For a sentence or short paragraph, yes. For long drafts, split the text and run each part separately — or consider Premium if you hit the cap daily.

How do I use each QuillBot feature?

Paraphrasing: paste text, pick a mode, tune the synonym slider. Summarizer: paste up to 1,200 words and choose the length. Grammar checker runs basic fixes. Translator and citation generator are fully unlocked on Premium.

How long can I use the free plan?

Indefinitely — it is not a trial. The limits are per pass (125 words) and per use (1,200-word summaries), not per month.

Can I get around paying for Premium?

The caps are per pass, so splitting text works for light use but gets tedious fast. For daily rewriting, the honest answer is the annual rate at $8.33/mo.
