What Each Plan Actually Does (From Our 30-Day Test)

We ran Writesonic for 30 days and stress-tested the platform. Here's the honest, plan-by-plan picture:

  • Starter ($79/mo annual): tracks whether your brand is cited in ChatGPT answers and flags citation gaps. In our test it found 3 citations, flagged 14 competitor gaps, and after we wrote 3 targeted articles, earned 1 new citation in Gemini. A taste — not a toolkit.
  • Basic ($199/mo annual): the first plan that covers multiple AI engines (ChatGPT + Gemini + AI Overviews) and two users. Reasonable for a small team running GEO alongside content.
  • Growth ($399/mo annual): where GEO becomes genuinely useful — sentiment analysis (is AI describing you well?) and the Action Center that prioritizes fixes. Only makes sense for teams or agencies operating at volume.

On the writing side, Writesonic's AI Article Writer still produces solid drafts fast — keyword to publish-ready draft averaged about 18 minutes. But raw prose trails ChatGPT and Claude, and coherence drops past 2,000 words. The writing is now the supporting act; the GEO platform is the show.
