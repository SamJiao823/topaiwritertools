The Free Plan Is Genuinely Free

Unlike most AI writing tools’ 7-day trials, Copy.ai’s free tier is permanent: 2,000 words per month, no credit card, 90+ templates. That’s roughly enough for 8–10 product descriptions or a handful of ad variations a month. If you write only occasionally, this is a legitimately usable free option — better than any free tier at Rytr’s 10,000 characters? No, but it’s a close second and it’s unlimited time.

What You Actually Pay For: Workflow Automation

Copy.ai’s differentiator is workflow automation — chaining research → writing → publishing into one pipeline. In our 30-day test, that genuinely helped GTM (go-to-market) teams that produce structured content repeatedly. The trade-off: raw writing quality is good but not best-in-class, and at $29–$1,000/mo it’s priced for teams, not individuals.

For a solo creator, the honest math is brutal: Rytr at $7.50/mo or Writesonic at $20/mo cover most short- and long-form needs at a fraction of Copy.ai’s price. You’re paying a big premium for the automation layer — and if you don’t need that layer, you shouldn’t pay it.

Our Verdict: Who Should Pay?

  • GTM / marketing teams producing repeatable content at volume — the workflow automation is real and saves hours.
  • Solo writers and freelancers — skip it. Rytr or Writesonic give you more writing value per dollar.
  • Occasional writers — use the free plan. 2,000 words/mo covers light work with zero cost.
