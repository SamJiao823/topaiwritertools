Writesonic Has No Free Tier — Here's What It Has Instead

Let's be direct: many people come to Writesonic expecting a free plan, because competitors have one. Rytr gives a forever-free tier with 10,000 characters a month. ChatGPT has a genuinely useful free layer. Writesonic's answer in 2026 is a trial, not a free tier — 7 days of full features, then a paid plan.

That's not necessarily a bad thing. A time-boxed trial of the real product tells you more than a throttled free tier ever will. But you should know the free alternatives before you sign up:

  • Rytr's forever-free plan: 10,000 characters/month with all templates and tones unlocked — solid for short-form, not for long-form pipelines.

Neither gives you Writesonic's Article Writer workflow or SERP analysis. That's the thing the trial is actually selling.
