How Do You Write Product Descriptions That Convert?

  1. Give the AI specifics: "a ceramic pour-over coffee dripper for a small kitchen" beats "a coffee product."
  2. Lead with the benefit: "Brews a cleaner cup in 3 minutes" beats "Premium ceramic material."
  3. Handle objections: Address sizing, shipping, and returns directly in the copy.
  4. Edit for your voice: Add a real customer quote or your own observation — AI output needs a human touch to convert.
  5. Structure for AI shopping agents: Clear specs first, benefits second, plus FAQ schema. AI search engines prioritize verifiable data.
